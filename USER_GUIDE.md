# LangSphere AI — End User Guide

Welcome to **LangSphere AI**, your intelligent companion for language learning! This guide walks you through all features and tools available in the platform.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Creating Your Account
1. Open the application at `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Click **Sign Up** on the welcome page.
3. Enter your full name, password, and an approved email address ending in `@gmail.com` or `@saveetha.com`.
4. Click **Create Account** to log in automatically.

### 2. Logging In
1. On the **Login** page, enter your registered email and password.
2. Check **Remember Me** to stay logged in across sessions.
3. Click **Sign In** to access your dashboard.

---

## 📚 Learning & Lessons

### Navigating the Learning Path
1. Click **My Lessons** from the sidebar or dashboard.
2. Select your target language (**English**, **Tamil**, **Hindi**, or **Telugu**).
3. Browse the structured units (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
4. Click any unlocked lesson node to open the **Lesson Detail Page**.

### Studying a Lesson
Inside a lesson, use the top tabs to navigate content:
- **Vocabulary**: View words, meanings, transliterations, and example sentences. Click the audio speaker icon 🔊 to hear native pronunciation via Speech Synthesis.
- **Grammar**: Study fundamental grammar rules and structural examples.
- **Examples**: Read natural contextual sentences.
- **Quiz**: Test your knowledge immediately.

### Completing a Lesson
Click **Mark as Complete** at the bottom of the lesson. You will receive an immediate XP and Coin reward added to your profile!

---

## 📖 Cultural Storybooks

1. Click **Stories** in the sidebar.
2. Select your preferred language tab (English, Tamil, Hindi, or Telugu).
3. Click on a story card to open the **Storybook Reader**.
4. Read page by page using the **Next Page** and **Previous Page** buttons.
5. Click **English** to toggle translations on or off.
6. Click the speaker icon 🔊 to listen to audio narration.

---

## 🧠 Quizzes & Assessment

1. Access quizzes directly from lesson pages or the quick access menu.
2. Read each question and select your chosen answer.
3. Click **Next Question** or **Finish Quiz**.
4. View your final score percentage, correct question breakdown, and earned XP/Coins on the results page.
5. Achieve a score of **70% or higher** to pass the quiz and automatically complete the linked lesson!

---

## 🤖 AI Tutor Chat

1. Click **Ask AI Tutor** or navigate to `/ai-tutor`.
2. Select your target language for conversation practice.
3. Type your message or grammar question in the chat input.
4. The AI Tutor will respond in your target language with helpful guidance and encouragement.
5. Your chat sessions and conversation history are saved automatically.

---

## ✍️ Handwriting Practice

1. Navigate to **Handwriting** (`/handwriting/ta`).
2. Use your mouse, touch, or stylus to draw the target character on the interactive canvas.
3. Use **Undo** to revert strokes or **Clear** to start over.
4. Click **Evaluate Handwriting** to receive AI feedback on stroke accuracy, precision score (0–100), and improvement tips.

---

## 📸 Vision OCR Scanner

1. Navigate to **Vision OCR** (`/vision-ocr`).
2. Upload an image containing printed or handwritten text.
3. Click **Extract Text**.
4. The OCR engine extracts text from the image, displaying original content and translation.

---

## 🛍️ Epic Shop & Customization

1. Click **Shop** in the sidebar.
2. Browse catalog categories: **Themes**, **Avatars**, **Frames**, **Titles**, **Boosters**, and **Badges**.
3. Use Coins earned from lessons and quizzes to purchase items.
4. Click **Equip** on owned items to update your profile title, avatar frame, or app appearance!

---

## 📊 Analytics Dashboard

Your **Smart Dashboard** displays:
- **Total XP**: Accumulated experience points.
- **Day Streak**: Consecutive days of active study.
- **Coins**: Virtual currency balance.
- **Level Progress**: Visual progress ring towards your next Scholar Level.
- **Activity Heatmap**: 7-day visualization of learning consistency.
- **AI Recommendations**: Tailored lesson suggestions.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Which email addresses are allowed?**  
A: Registration is restricted to `@gmail.com` and `@saveetha.com` addresses.

**Q: How do I earn Coins?**  
A: Completing lessons awards 7–20 coins. Passing quizzes awards up to 25 coins based on your score percentage.

**Q: Can I retake a quiz?**  
A: Yes! You can retake quizzes anytime to improve your score and earn additional XP.
