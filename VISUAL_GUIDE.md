## 🎨 Color Scheme

### Primary Colors
- **Forest Green**: `#22c55e` - Main theme color
- **Magic Purple**: `#a855f7` - Accent color
- **Sky Blue**: `#3b82f6` - Supporting color

### Reward Colors
- **Gold**: `#FFD700` - Achievements
- **Diamond**: `#B9F2FF` - Currency
- **XP Orange**: `#FF6B35` - Experience points
- **Streak Red**: `#ef4444` - Streak flame

### Gradients
- Forest: `from-green-400 to-emerald-500`
- Magic: `from-purple-400 to-pink-500`
- Ocean: `from-blue-400 to-cyan-500`
- Sunset: `from-orange-400 to-red-500`

## ✨ Animation Effects

### Hover Effects
- **Scale Up**: 1.05x on hover
- **Shadow Grow**: Larger shadow on hover
- **Color Shift**: Gradient transitions

### Click Effects
- **Scale Down**: 0.95x on click
- **Ripple**: Expanding circle
- **Bounce**: Spring animation

### Page Transitions
- **Fade In**: Opacity 0 → 1
- **Slide Up**: Y: 20px → 0
- **Scale In**: Scale 0.9 → 1

### Special Animations
- **Flying Bird**: Bezier curve path
- **Floating Leaves**: Continuous fall
- **Sparkle**: Pulsing glow
- **Progress Bar**: Smooth fill
